Professional Summary

Research Engineer with 18+ years of academic and industrial experience developing optical metrology, materials-characterization, and AI-driven analytical methods across packaging, semiconductor, photovoltaic, aerospace, and flexible-electronics applications.

Deep specialization in experimental, non-destructive optical stress and deformation measurement — digital photoelasticity, digital image correlation (DIC), NIR polariscopy, and phase-shifting interferometry — applied to PET/rPET/PEF packaging, semiconductor-grade silicon wafers, solar cells, carbon-fiber composites, and flexible electronics.

Technical-lead track record on industry-sponsored programs with Coca-Cola, Sidel, ALPLA, Plastic Technologies Inc., Boeing, NextFlex, SunPower, and the Photovoltaic Manufacturing Consortium. Currently building AI/computer-vision frameworks to automate interpretation of optical fringe and deformation data, extending processing-structure-performance models to recycled and bio-based materials.

Author of 20+ peer-reviewed publications and conference papers; certified peer reviewer for Elsevier and IOP journals.
